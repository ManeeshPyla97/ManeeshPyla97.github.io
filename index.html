About

I study how low-energy electrons attach to molecules and cause damage. Using single- and multireference wave-function methods, I characterize electronic resonances (their energies, widths and decay channels) in heterocycles and solvated biomolecules.

I am advised by Prof. Spiridoula Matsika at Temple University in Philadelphia. My work so far has produced four peer-reviewed papers (two first-author) and two journal covers. I am currently extending it toward machine-learning-accelerated resonance methods, core-excited resonances and non-adiabatic dynamics.
